Status: fixed on 19 November 2026, before the recorded trials of this protocol.

## Why a fourth content protocol

On 19 November 2026, a change of both SDKs made a call faster without a change of the frame format, of a rule, or of a verdict ([comparison protocol 5](../comparison/PROTOCOL-5.md), section 2). The change touches the Python frame decoder and the client and owner of both SDKs, which carry every spike and sample of NEST.
Content protocol 4 runs every trial of [content protocol 3](PROTOCOL-CONTENT-3.md) again with the changed SDKs.

## What changes

1. The SDKs: revision 7 with the speed changes, at the commit that the schedule records.
2. The order of each block is shuffled with the seed 20261121.

The runner is `content_run.py --protocol 4`, and the analysis is `analyze_content3.py`.

## What is the same as content protocol 3

Everything else: NEST and its version, the eight networks of seven neuron models, the configurations and their numbers of trials (216 in three blocks), the owner and both hosts, the bitwise comparison of every spike, offset, and membrane-potential sample, the self-test of the comparison, and the hypotheses with their criteria.
