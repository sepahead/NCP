## Why a fifth protocol

[Protocol 4](PROTOCOL-4.md) started on 2 November 2026 at 00:13 UTC, after a restart of the computer had removed the limit of about 3.1 GHz that stopped it in October.
It was stopped at 00:19 UTC after 43 attempts of 33 configurations of block 0.
Each attempt started with a core at 4.009 to 4.491 GHz, on the power adapter, at thermal pressure 0, with the load average below 8.
11 attempts were marked as disturbed. The only reason was the clock at the end of the run: 3.962 to 3.999 GHz, against the limit of 4.0 GHz.
No attempt slept, ran on battery, or had a thermal pressure other than 0.
Seven of the 11 were attempts of the NCP 1.0.1 Rust host, whose long runs keep the processor busy for longer. One configuration of that host was disturbed in all three attempts and would leave the timing measures.

The limit of 4.0 GHz was set to detect the limit of about 3.1 GHz.
Readings slightly below 4.0 GHz just after a long run are normal frequency scaling of this computer, not that limit.
With the limit of 4.0 GHz, the long runs of the slower arms would leave the timing measures more often than the others.
That would remove exactly the comparisons that hypothesis G6 needs.

## What changes

One condition changes. A run is disturbed when, at its end, a core runs below **3.8 GHz** (protocol 4: 4.0 GHz).
The conditions before each run do not change: the runner still waits for a core at 4.0 GHz or more.
3.8 GHz is 0.7 GHz above the limit of about 3.1 GHz and 0.16 GHz below the lowest reading of the 43 attempts.

## What is the same as protocol 4

Everything else is as in protocol 4 and the protocols that it refers to: the computer, the NEST build, the networks, the network seed 20261024 + b of block b, the parts D1 to D4, the arms, the 258 configurations, three blocks, the order seed 20261027, the measures, the time of each step, the admissibility of a block, the hypotheses G1 to G7, and what is not claimed.
The runner is `run4.py --protocol 5`, and the analysis is `analyze4.py`.

The 43 attempts of protocol 4 are kept as a pilot record. They are not runs of this protocol, and the analysis does not use them.
