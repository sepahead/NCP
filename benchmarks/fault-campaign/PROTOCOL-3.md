# Fault campaign: protocol 3, for NCP 2

Status: fixed on 2 November 2026, before the recorded trials of this protocol.
Its SHA-256 is in [`PROTOCOL-3.sha256`](PROTOCOL-3.sha256). The schedule records the same digest.
Do not edit this file. Record any deviation in the README.

## Why a third protocol

[Protocol 2](PROTOCOL-2.md) ran on 2 November 2026 from 20:04 to 20:17 UTC. Its trials held E1 to E8, and E9 failed in one sample (README).
After the runs, its records showed a defect of the harness. The client chose the frame to change with `random.Random(seed)`, and the relay drew the bit or the cut with a new `random.Random(seed)` from the same seed.
The two first draws came from the same random number, so a flipped bit or a cut fell into the part of the frame's range that the frame's index selected, unless the relay's first draw fell beyond the end of the frame.
All 80 flips in a header hit the `open` request or response, and no flip hit the header of a step.
Protocol 2 says that one random bit anywhere in the frame is flipped; its trials did not do that.

E9 of protocol 2 failed because the last sample of one soak fell while the owner exited, after the client had finished all its calls: the owner's open descriptors read 1 instead of 6.
The criterion did not say what to do with a sample taken while the processes exit.

## What changes

1. The client draws a seed for the relay from its own random generator, after it has drawn the frame, and passes that seed to the relay. The relay's draws are then independent of the frame's index.
2. E9 leaves the last sample of each soak out of the check of the open descriptors. The memory criterion keeps every sample.
3. The order of each block is shuffled with the seed 20261104.

## What is the same as protocol 2

Everything else: the client, the owners, the execution logs, the scenarios and their parameters, the plan of 4,170 trials and 9 soaks in three blocks, the views, the invariants, the hypotheses E1 to E9 and their decision rules (with the change to E9 above), and what the results do not show.
The runner is `run2.py --protocol 3`, and the analysis is `analyze2.py`, which also counts the flipped bits by frame and region.

## Smoke runs that informed this protocol

On 2 November 2026, before this protocol was fixed, the corrected harness ran the smoke plan of protocol 2: one trial of each scenario and owner, and three short soaks.
Every trial ran and held every invariant. The four flipped bits fell into the header of frame 0, the JSON region of frames 3 and 5, and the attachments of frame 6. The soaks were too short for ten samples.
These runs are not results.
