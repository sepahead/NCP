# Fault campaign: protocol 8, protocol 6 with the speed changes of the SDKs

Status: fixed on 19 November 2026, before the recorded trials of this protocol.
Its SHA-256 is in [`PROTOCOL-8.sha256`](PROTOCOL-8.sha256). The schedule records the same digest.
Do not edit this file. Record any deviation in the README.

## Why an eighth protocol

On 19 November 2026, a change of both SDKs made a call faster without a change of the frame format, of a rule, or of a verdict ([comparison protocol 5](../comparison/PROTOCOL-5.md), section 2). The change touches the code of the client and of the owner that the fault campaign tests: the transitions of their state, their events, and the Python frame decoder.
The migration plan requires the fault campaign again after a change of the SDKs. Protocol 8 runs every trial of [protocol 6](PROTOCOL-6.md) again with the changed SDKs.

## What changes

1. The SDKs: revision 7 with the speed changes, at the commit that the schedule records.
2. The order of each block is shuffled with the seed 20261121, so the trials draw new kill times, frames, and bits.

The runner is `run4.py --protocol 8`, and the analysis is `analyze4.py`.

## What is the same as protocol 6

Everything else: the harness, the bindings (Unix domain sockets and TCP), the clients (Python and Rust), the owners (Python and Rust), the relay, the session of open, six steps, and close, the scenarios and their numbers of trials (4,560 in three blocks), the execution log, the invariants, the view of a stalled step, and the hypotheses E1 to E9 with their criteria.
