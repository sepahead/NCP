# Fault campaign: protocol 5, protocol 4 with a corrected cleanup

Status: fixed on 9 November 2026, before the recorded trials of this protocol.
Its SHA-256 is in [`PROTOCOL-5.sha256`](PROTOCOL-5.sha256). The schedule records the same digest.
Do not edit this file. Record any deviation in the README.

## Why a fifth protocol

[Protocol 4](PROTOCOL-4.md) ran on 9 November 2026 from 19:16 to 19:30 UTC, from the clean commit `7fb3df8`.
Of its 4,560 scheduled trials, 4,554 ended with a result, and none of them broke a criterion of E1 to E8.
Six trials ended without a result. All six were `kill-owner` trials with the Rust owner, which runs as a server; five ran over a Unix domain socket and one over TCP.
In each, the trial's timer had killed the owner, and the driver's cleanup ([`harness/fault_client4.py`](harness/fault_client4.py), `stop`) then sent SIGTERM to the owner's process group.
When the owner had died but had not yet been reaped, macOS answered with `EPERM`: `killpg` on a process group whose only member is a zombie fails with "Operation not permitted", while `kill` on the same process succeeds, and `killpg` after the reaping fails with `ESRCH`.
The driver caught only `ESRCH` (`ProcessLookupError`), so it stopped before it printed the trial's result.
Protocol 4 counts a trial without a result against every hypothesis, so formally none of E1 to E8 holds.

The defect is in the harness, not in NCP: it struck after the session had ended, in the cleanup of the owner process.

## What changes

1. The driver's cleanup treats `PermissionError` from `killpg` like `ProcessLookupError`: the process group is already dying, and the driver waits for the owner to exit.
2. The order of each block is shuffled with the seed 20261109 (protocol 4: 20261105), so the trials draw new kill times, frames, and bits.

The runner is `run4.py --protocol 5`, and the analysis is `analyze4.py`.

## What is the same as protocol 4

Everything else: the bindings, the clients, the owners, the relay, the session, the scenarios and their numbers of trials (4,560 in three blocks), the execution log, the views, the invariants, the hypotheses E1 to E8 with their criteria, the rule that a hypothesis holds only if every scheduled trial ran, and what the results do not show.
The trials measure no time except the detection of a stall, so they need no controlled conditions; the computer may run on battery, and the schedule records the power source.

## Smoke runs that informed this protocol

On 9 November 2026, before this protocol was fixed, a test on this computer reproduced the behavior of macOS: a process started in its own session and killed with SIGKILL gave `PermissionError` for `killpg` while it was a zombie, no error for `kill`, and `ProcessLookupError` for `killpg` after `poll` had reaped it.
A smoke run of `run4.py --protocol 5` with one trial of each of the 88 combinations of scenario, owner, binding, and client ran every trial, and every criterion held.
These runs are not results.
